# Corsarium

Set sail into the nanometric world of biological macromolecules.

Corsarium is an open educational platform for learning bioinformatics, with a special focus on structural bioinformatics. It is built for every corsair adventurer willing to embark on a journey into the vast, unseen world of biological macromolecules.

From protein structures and molecular visualization to molecular dynamics, computational methods, and structural analysis, Corsarium aims to make the tools and concepts of structural bioinformatics accessible to curious minds willing to explore the molecular world.
